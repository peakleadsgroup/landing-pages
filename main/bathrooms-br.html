Frequently Asked Questions

What is the cost of a bathroom remodel? ▼
Costs vary based on project scope. Tub-to-shower conversions typically range from $10,000-$22,000+, while tub replacements range from $9,000-$20,000+. Get a free quote for an accurate estimate.
How long does a bathroom remodel take? ▼
Most shower and tub projects can be completed in 1-2 days. Full bathroom remodels may take 1-2 weeks depending on scope.
Can I use my bathroom during the remodel? ▼
For shower or tub replacements, you may need to use an alternative bathroom for 24-48 hours. We'll discuss your specific situation during the estimate.
What materials do you offer? ▼
We offer acrylic, solid surface, and other premium materials. We do not install tile. Our team can help you choose the best option for your needs and budget.
Should I replace or reface my tub? ▼
If your tub is damaged, outdated, or you want a different style, replacement is often the best option. Refacing may be suitable for minor updates—we can assess during your free estimate.
How can I maximize my bathroom storage? ▼
We offer custom vanities, shelving solutions, and built-in storage options. Our team will work with you to design a layout that maximizes your space.
