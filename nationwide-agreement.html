Nationwide Service Agreement

By signing below and saving a payment method, you ("Client") enter a binding agreement with Peak Leads Group ("PLG") for the Nationwide $49 exclusive lead product.

1. Services provided

PLG will deliver qualified homeowner bathroom leads (shower replacement specific) generated through targeted advertising and landing pages. Leads are exclusive once sent to you (not resold).

2. Product structure & pricing

  • Price: $49 per exclusive qualified bathroom lead (flat).
  • Quality: Same exact ads and landing page as our dedicated $99-$150 product.
  • Billing: No upfront payment. Card on file only. Your card is charged only upon lead delivery. No management fee and no monthly retainer.
  • Volume: Not guaranteed. This is not a dedicated market campaign and does not promise a set number of leads in your area.
  • Launch: We'll launch this new model as soon as we have enough zip code coverage across the US - likely in a couple of weeks.

3. Qualified leads

A Qualified Lead meets all of the following:

  • Verified homeowner - as determined by the onboarding intake form on the landing page
  • Zip code provided is within the agreed-upon service area
  • Includes a phone number that is working and in service
  • Includes a valid email address, as determined by an online email verification tool

If a lead does not meet these criteria, it can be reported to PLG within 7 days of delivery for review. If confirmed, it will be replaced free of charge.

4. Lead delivery & communication

Qualified leads will be sent to the client's chosen contact method. It is the client's responsibility to respond promptly to leads to ensure successful outcomes.

5. Service area

You provide your service area (cities, ZIPs, and/or radius). PLG routes matching leads when available within that area. You may update your service area by contacting PLG in writing.

For returning partners, PLG will use the zip codes on file from your previous campaign unless you contact us in writing to update them.

6. Cancellation policy

Either party may cancel this agreement at any time for any reason, provided the Client pays any outstanding balance owed for leads delivered up to the date of cancellation. Upon cancellation, lead delivery will cease within 24 hours. The Client remains responsible for paying for any qualified leads that are sent during that 24-hour period.

7. Payment terms

Client agrees to maintain a valid payment method on file and authorizes PLG to charge that method automatically for each qualified lead sent. If a charge fails or is declined, lead delivery will be immediately paused until the issue is resolved. Repeated payment failures may result in termination of this agreement.

8. Entire agreement

This Agreement constitutes the full understanding between the Client and PLG for the Nationwide product. No other promises or agreements shall be valid unless made in writing and signed by both parties. Volume is not guaranteed. No dedicated-campaign volume commitments apply to this product.

9. Limitation of liability

PLG shall not be held liable for any indirect, incidental, special, or consequential damages arising out of or related to this Agreement, including but not limited to lost profits or missed opportunities. PLG's total liability under this Agreement shall not exceed the total amount paid by the Client during the term of the Agreement.

10. Confidentiality

Both parties agree to keep all proprietary or confidential information shared during the term of this Agreement strictly confidential and not disclose it to any third party without prior written consent, unless required by law.

11. Dispute resolution

In the event of a dispute, both parties agree to attempt to resolve the matter through good faith negotiations. If a resolution cannot be reached, the parties agree to submit the matter to binding arbitration in the state of North Carolina.

12. Electronic agreement

The Client acknowledges that by signing electronically and submitting a payment method through Stripe or another digital platform, they are entering into a legally binding agreement equivalent to a signed written contract.
