Lead Generation Revenue Share Agreement

This Lead Generation Revenue Share Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of October 30, 2025, by and between:

Peak Leads Group, LLC ("Agency"), a marketing and lead generation company represented by Daniel Wellish, and Bath Pro Carolinas ("Client") represented by Steven Krol

Together referred to as the "Parties," and individually as a "Party."

1. Purpose

Agency agrees to provide exclusive lead generation and marketing services for Client's bath remodeling business, and Client agrees to compensate Agency through a revenue share model based on closed sales directly resulting from Agency-provided leads.

2. Scope of Services

Agency shall:

  • Generate and deliver leads for bath remodeling services to Client through digital advertising and related marketing channels.
  • Provide leads via email.

Client shall:

  • Accurately report all resulting sales from Agency-provided leads.

2A. Lead Attribution Definition

For the purpose of calculating revenue share, a "lead generated by Agency" is defined as any individual that:

  • Submits contact information (name, phone, email, or address) through an advertising campaign, landing page, or form owned or operated by Agency.
  • Engages with Agency-generated marketing content (including ads, forms, or phone calls) resulting in direct contact with Client.
  • Contacts Client within 90 days of their initial interaction with Agency's campaign, provided their source can be verified via ad platform data, call tracking, form submission timestamps, or CRM tagging.

3. Compensation and Payment Terms

a. Revenue Share: Client agrees to pay Agency fifteen percent (15%) of the total sale value for all closed deals that originate from leads generated by Agency.

b. Payment Schedule: Payments are due within seven (7) calendar days of Client marking the deal "closed won" in their CRM. Payments shall be made via ACH, wire transfer, or other agreed method to Agency's designated account.

c. Audit Rights: Agency reserves the right to reasonably verify sales reports through mutually agreed documentation or CRM access to ensure transparency.

d. CRM Access: Client agrees to grant Agency read-only access to its CRM or sales tracking system for the duration of this Agreement and for thirty (30) days following termination if applicable. This access is solely for verifying lead attribution, sales status, and accurate revenue share calculations. Agency agrees to maintain the confidentiality of all information accessed through Client's CRM and to use it exclusively for the purposes described herein.

e. Post-Termination Revenue Share: Client agrees that any deals closed during the 30-day CRM access period following termination of this Agreement shall still be subject to the fifteen percent (15%) revenue share owed to Agency, regardless of the Agreement being voided. Payment for such deals is due within seven (7) calendar days of Client marking the deal "closed won" in their CRM.

4. Term and Termination

a. Term: This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and continue until termination with 24 hours notice.

b. Termination for Cause: Either Party may terminate immediately if the other Party materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure such breach within 24 hours written notice.

c. Effect of Termination: Client shall remain responsible for any revenue share payments owed to Agency for sales closed prior to the termination date, as well as those closed during the 30-day CRM access period (see Section 3e).

5. Exclusivity

Agency agrees to generate leads exclusively for Client in the agreed-upon service area for bath remodeling during the term of this Agreement. Client agrees not to share Agency-provided leads with any third party or other business entity.

6. Confidentiality

Each Party agrees to maintain the confidentiality of all non-public information shared during this Agreement, including lead data, pricing, marketing strategies, and client/customer information.

7. Limitation of Liability

Agency shall not be liable for indirect, incidental, or consequential damages. Agency makes no guarantee of specific revenue or profit levels, though it commits to delivering agreed-upon lead volumes and quality standards.

8. Governing Law and Venue

This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of North Carolina, without regard to its conflict of law principles. Any disputes shall be resolved in the courts of Wake County, North Carolina.

9. Entire Agreement

This Agreement constitutes the entire understanding between the Parties with respect to the subject matter and supersedes all prior agreements or understandings, written or oral.
