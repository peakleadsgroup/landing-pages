Lead Generation Revenue Share Agreement

This Lead Generation Revenue Share Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of October 30, 2025, by and between:

Peak Leads Group, LLC ("Agency")

A marketing and lead generation company represented by Daniel Wellish

and

Bath Pro Carolinas ("Client")

Represented by Steven Krol

Collectively referred to as the "Parties" and individually as a "Party."

1. Purpose

Agency agrees to generate exclusive bath remodeling leads for Client, and Client agrees to compensate Agency through a revenue share based on closed sales directly resulting from Agency-generated leads.

2. Scope of Services

Agency shall:

  • Generate and deliver qualified leads for bath remodeling services through digital advertising and related marketing channels.
  • Deliver leads to Client primarily via email or other agreed delivery method.

Client shall:

  • Accurately track and report all sales resulting from Agency-provided leads.
  • Maintain transparent sales records for verification purposes.

2A. Lead Attribution and Ownership

For the purposes of calculating revenue share, a "lead generated by Agency" is defined as any individual who:

  • Submits contact information (name, phone, email, or address) through a campaign, landing page, or form owned or operated by Agency; or
  • Engages with Agency-created marketing content (including ads, forms, or tracked phone calls) that results in direct contact with Client; or
  • Contacts Client within ninety (90) days of their initial interaction with an Agency campaign, provided attribution can be verified through ad platform data, form timestamps, email delivery logs, or CRM tagging.

In the event a lead was generated by more than one agency or source, the Parties agree that credit shall be granted only to the agency that first submitted the lead to Client, as evidenced by email delivery timestamps or comparable transmission records. Leads delivered after another source's timestamped submission shall not qualify for revenue share under this Agreement.

3. Compensation and Payment Terms

a. Revenue Share: Client agrees to pay Agency fifteen percent (15%) of the total sale value for all closed deals originating from Agency-generated leads.

b. Payment Schedule: Payments are due within seven (7) calendar days of Client receiving payment from the end customer. Payments shall be made via ACH, wire transfer, or another mutually agreed method to Agency's designated account.

c. Audit Rights: Agency may reasonably verify sales reports through documentation or limited CRM access to ensure accuracy.

d. CRM Access: Client grants Agency read-only CRM access during this Agreement and for thirty (30) days following termination, solely to verify lead attribution, sales status, and revenue share calculations. Agency shall maintain confidentiality of all accessed data.

e. Post-Termination Revenue Share: Deals closed within the 30-day CRM access period following termination remain subject to the fifteen percent (15%) revenue share. Payment is due within seven (7) calendar days of Client receiving funds.

4. Term and Termination

a. Term: This Agreement shall begin on the Effective Date and continue until terminated by either Party with twenty-four (24) hours' written notice.

b. Termination for Cause: Either Party may terminate immediately if the other materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure such breach within twenty-four (24) hours of written notice.

c. Effect of Termination: Client remains responsible for revenue share owed on all deals closed prior to termination and those closed within the post-termination CRM access period (Section 3e).

5. Exclusivity

Agency agrees to generate leads exclusively for Client in the agreed-upon service area during the term of this Agreement. Client shall not share Agency-provided leads with third parties or competing entities.

6. Confidentiality

Both Parties agree to maintain confidentiality regarding all non-public information, including lead data, pricing, marketing methods, and customer information.

7. Limitation of Liability

Agency shall not be liable for indirect, incidental, or consequential damages. No guarantee of specific sales or profit levels is made.

8. Governing Law and Venue

This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of North Carolina. Any disputes shall be resolved in the courts of Wake County, North Carolina.

9. Entire Agreement

This document represents the full and final understanding between the Parties and supersedes all prior discussions or agreements.
