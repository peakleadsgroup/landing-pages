Service Agreement

Pay Per Result Service Agreement

This Service Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into by and between Peak Leads Group ("PLG") and Baths R Us ("Client"). By submitting payment for the onboarding invoice, the Client acknowledges and agrees to the terms set forth in this Agreement, and Peak Leads Group agrees to uphold its responsibilities as outlined herein. Payment constitutes mutual acceptance of this Agreement, including the services, responsibilities, and billing terms described below.

1
Services Provided

PLG will deliver qualified homeowner leads through targeted Facebook and Instagram ad campaigns.

2
Partnership Structure

Lead Billing – $99 per lead

Client will be billed weekly via credit card for all qualified leads delivered during the prior week. Charges will be processed automatically each week based on the number of qualified leads delivered in the preceding seven (7) days. By providing and maintaining a valid credit card on file, the Client authorizes PLG to process these weekly charges automatically in accordance with this Agreement.

3
Qualified Leads

A Qualified Lead meets all of the following:

  • Verified homeowner or businessowner as determined by the onboarding intake form on the landing page
  • Zip code provided is within the agreed-upon service area
  • Includes a phone number that is working and in service
  • Is not a duplicate of any lead previously delivered by PLG to Client within the preceding ninety (90) days, as determined by matching phone number or email address. A lead sharing the same phone number or email address as any lead delivered within the prior 90-day period shall not qualify as a Qualified Lead and shall be replaced at no charge upon Client's written notice to PLG within 7 days of delivery.

If a lead does not meet these criteria, it can be reported to PLG within 7 days of delivery for review. If confirmed, it will be replaced free of charge.

4
Lead Delivery & Communication

Qualified leads will be delivered to the client's chosen contact method. It is the client's responsibility to respond promptly to leads to ensure successful outcomes. The client agrees to receive a minimum of 30 qualified leads per month for the duration of their agreement with Peak Leads Group.

5
Cancellation Policy

Either party may cancel this agreement at any time for any reason, provided the Client pays any outstanding balance owed for leads delivered up to the date of cancellation. Upon cancellation, lead delivery will cease within 24 hours. The Client remains responsible for paying for any qualified leads that are delivered during that 24-hour period.

6
Payment Terms

Client agrees to maintain a valid payment method on file. If a charge fails or is declined, lead delivery will be immediately paused until the issue is resolved. Repeated payment failures may result in termination of this agreement.

7
TCPA Compliance and Indemnification

PLG represents, warrants, and covenants that all leads delivered to Client under this Agreement shall be generated and obtained in full compliance with the Telephone Consumer Protection Act, 47 U.S.C. § 227 ("TCPA"), and all applicable implementing regulations, including but not limited to FCC rules and any state-law equivalents (collectively, "TCPA Requirements"). Without limiting the foregoing, PLG warrants that:

Each lead has provided prior express written consent, as defined under the TCPA, to be contacted by telephone, text message, or other covered means, specifically authorizing contact from Client or its agents regarding Client's products and services;

Consent was obtained through lawful means, with clear and conspicuous disclosure identifying Client (or PLG on Client's behalf) as a party authorized to contact the consumer;

PLG has not obtained consent through deceptive, misleading, or unlawful practices; and

PLG maintains, and will make available to Client upon written request, documented proof of consent (including timestamp, IP address, consent language, and landing page URL) for each lead delivered; and

PLG shall retain for each Lead the following information as proof of such consent:

  • (a) IP address of the user submitting the form;
  • (b) Date and time of submission (timestamp);
  • (c) Source URL where the consent was obtained;
  • (d) Full opt-in language presented to the user at the time of submission;
  • (e) Indication of the user's affirmative action (e.g., checkbox or button click).

PLG will maintain this data for a minimum period of five (5) years from the date the Lead was generated and shall make such data available to the Client within five (5) business days.

Client shall promptly notify PLG in writing of any TCPA-related claim or demand for which indemnification may be sought. PLG shall have the right to assume control of the defense of any such claim, provided that Client shall have the right to participate with counsel of its own choosing at Client's expense.
